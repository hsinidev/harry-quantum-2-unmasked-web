What is Harry Quantum 2 Unmasked Web?

Hello, players! Welcome to Harry Quantum 2 Unmasked Web, a browser-based physics-based puzzle game created by NetEase. In this game, you'll navigate your character through various levels, collecting items and solving puzzles while avoiding obstacles.

How to Play

  • Clock the time as much as possible in each level by timing your movements correctly.

  • Collect as many energy orbs as you can within a certain amount of time to unlock rewards and upgrade your character.

  • Solve puzzles by using physics to manipulate objects and reach new levels or access hidden areas.

Controls and Objective

Your goal is to navigate your character through increasingly complex levels while collecting energy orbs and avoiding obstacles. The game features a simple control scheme: use the mouse wheel to clock the time, move left and right using the arrow keys or WASD keys, and use the space bar to interact with objects.

Tips and Tricks for Success

  • Use gravity to your advantage by timing jumps and dodges correctly to avoid obstacles.

  • Build momentum by collecting energy orbs in one spot before moving on to the next level.

  • Solve puzzles quickly, as each solution will unlock new areas or provide a time bonus.
